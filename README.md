# Sum up 26 November's world news in one image

A newspaper front page for Thursday, 26 November 2026: six stories from the day, drawn at build time and committed as `entry.png`. The masthead names the day; each panel pairs a pictogram with a headline and a one-line summary, so a reader who missed the day can tell what happened from the image alone.

## The day's news (26 November 2026)

- **Oil shock** — Houthis claim an attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery; Brent crude tops $94 a barrel.
- **First Pakistani in orbit** — an astronaut joins China's Shenzhou-24 crew aboard the Tiangong station.
- **Mercury, at last** — BepiColombo enters orbit after an eight-year journey.
- **Chess: deadlock** — Gukesh v Sindarov, Game 2 of the World Chess Championship ends in a 20-move draw.
- **Tariffs** — the US proposes a 15% tariff on Taiwan, with new levies on Japan, China and the EU.
- **Cairo tragedy** — a fuel tanker crash and fire near Cairo kills at least 30 people.

## Entry

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` (1024×1024, 86,086 bytes) to `/output/entry.png`. Standard library only, no network, no third-party packages, byte-identical every run.
